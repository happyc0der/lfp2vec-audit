# Electrode position and what the signal adds to it

Generated by `lfpaudit position-report` from the tables under `results/`. Balanced
accuracy on held-out sessions, four-class view, logistic regression throughout.

## The honest baseline, and the leak it replaces

Position is absolute depth along the shank plus lateral offset, both known from the
probe's geometry before anything is recorded. An earlier version of this repository
also
used depth rescaled to the span of channels kept on each probe. Kept channels are
chosen
by histology label, so that span encodes the test probe's own anatomy. The second
row is
that feature on its own, kept here only so the size of the error is on record.

| features | within IBL | within Allen | IBL → Allen | Allen → IBL |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| *chance* | *0.298* | *0.358* | *0.358* | *0.298* |
| **electrode position (honest)** | 0.773 | 0.491 | 0.233 | 0.298 |
| relative depth within labelled span *(leaks test labels)* | 0.831 | 0.829 | 0.810 | 0.764 |

Honest position is strong where insertions are stereotyped, modest where they are
not,
and at or below chance across labs. The leaky feature transfers across labs almost
perfectly because it is, in effect, a lookup into the answer.

## What the signal adds to position

Each source gets its own classifier and the two are fused as a product of experts:
class log-probabilities added, the class prior subtracted once. Nothing is fitted
on top.
Experts here are trained on a class-balanced sample of at most 7 500 chunks per
class,
so single-source numbers differ slightly from the full-data table above.

| source | within IBL | within Allen | IBL → Allen | Allen → IBL |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| position | 0.751 | 0.514 | 0.199 | 0.298 |
| band power | 0.419 | 0.603 | 0.371 | 0.371 |
| frozen audio model | 0.679 | 0.704 | 0.368 | 0.300 |
| position × band power | 0.734 | 0.625 | 0.265 | 0.531 |
| position × frozen audio model | 0.775 | 0.717 | 0.350 | 0.309 |

- **within IBL**: fusing adds +0.023 to position (4 of 7 sessions) and +0.095 to the signal (7 of 7).
- **within Allen**: fusing adds +0.202 to position (6 of 10 sessions) and +0.012 to the signal (6 of 10).

The two sources are complementary. Where insertions are stereotyped position leads
and
the signal adds a little; where they are not, the signal leads and position adds a
little. Across labs neither full-band source transfers, which is the subject of
`RESULTS_FIXES.md`. The one large cross-lab cell, position × band power from Allen to
IBL, does not replicate in the other direction and is not a finding.

## How well must insertion depth be known?

The whole held-out probe is shifted along its shank by a uniform random offset, ten
draws per level, and the position model is trained on probes shifted the same way
so it
has learned how far to trust depth. The signal never sees depth.

![depth uncertainty](figures/depth_uncertainty.png)

**within IBL**

| depth uncertainty | position | signal | fused |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| exact | 0.750 | 0.679 | 0.775 |
| ±100 µm | 0.746 | 0.679 | 0.772 |
| ±200 µm | 0.713 | 0.679 | 0.769 |
| ±400 µm | 0.627 | 0.679 | 0.741 |
| ±800 µm | 0.469 | 0.679 | 0.702 |
| ±1600 µm | 0.410 | 0.679 | 0.686 |

The signal becomes the better single source beyond about ±280 µm,
and the fusion is at or above both at every level.

**within Allen**

| depth uncertainty | position | signal | fused |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| exact | 0.514 | 0.703 | 0.717 |
| ±100 µm | 0.516 | 0.703 | 0.716 |
| ±200 µm | 0.521 | 0.703 | 0.718 |
| ±400 µm | 0.525 | 0.703 | 0.715 |
| ±800 µm | 0.496 | 0.703 | 0.710 |
| ±1600 µm | 0.436 | 0.703 | 0.716 |

The signal is the better single source at every level, exact depth included.

## Where along the probe: a hypothesis that failed

The expectation was that position would fail first near anatomical boundaries, whose
depth varies between animals, and that the signal would rescue it there. It does not.
Raw accuracy against distance to the nearest boundary on the same probe:

**within IBL**

| distance to boundary | chunks | position | signal | fused |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0–50 µm | 19900 | 0.643 | 0.575 | 0.645 |
| 50–100 µm | 28300 | 0.710 | 0.651 | 0.740 |
| 100–200 µm | 41700 | 0.818 | 0.772 | 0.868 |
| 200–400 µm | 41300 | 0.891 | 0.828 | 0.934 |
| ≥400 µm | 1400 | 1.000 | 0.998 | 1.000 |

**within Allen**

| distance to boundary | chunks | position | signal | fused |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0–50 µm | 5900 | 0.525 | 0.596 | 0.604 |
| 50–100 µm | 5800 | 0.517 | 0.664 | 0.664 |
| 100–200 µm | 15600 | 0.513 | 0.728 | 0.736 |
| 200–400 µm | 19200 | 0.594 | 0.777 | 0.795 |
| ≥400 µm | 3300 | 0.606 | 0.891 | 0.953 |

Every source degrades together as a boundary approaches, and the gain from fusion is
larger away from boundaries than at them. Histological labels are also least
certain at
boundaries, so part of this is label noise that no source could overcome.
